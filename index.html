surf-sort

After a boat trip you get handed a few thousand photos from the resort photographer, mixed across every surfer on the boat. Nobody wants to scroll through all of them to find their own. surf-sort splits them by person automatically.

YOLO finds the surfer in the frame, the crop goes through InsightFace for a face embedding, and agglomerative clustering groups the embeddings by cosine distance. Anything ambiguous lands in a manual-review folder instead of the wrong album.
